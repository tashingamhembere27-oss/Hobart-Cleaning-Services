House cleaning cost: common questions

How much do house cleaners charge in Hobart?

We charge flat rates by home size. A once-off house clean starts at $132 for a studio or 1-bedroom home and $165 for 2 bedrooms, and regular weekly and fortnightly cleans cost less per visit. See the price list above or use the calculator for your home.

Do you charge per hour?

No. Our house cleaning price is a flat rate based on the size of your home, with a minimum time on site so the job is done properly.

How much does end of lease cleaning cost?

End of lease cleaning in Hobart starts from $440 for a 1-bedroom home, $550 for 2 bedrooms, $660 for 3 bedrooms and $990 for 4 bedrooms, with our Bond Back Guarantee.

How much does a spring clean or deep clean cost?

Spring cleaning starts from $231 for a studio or 1-bedroom home, $297 for 2 bedrooms, $363 for 3 bedrooms and $462 for 4 bedrooms.

Can I book a once-off cleaning service?

Yes. Choose “One time” in the price calculator and book online, or call us on 0458 283 459. You can also set up a regular clean every 4 weeks, fortnightly or weekly.

Where do you work?

We are a Hobart, Tasmania cleaning company and we travel to your home.
